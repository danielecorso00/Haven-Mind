How Haven Mind Works

Collaborative Safety Net

Institutional Ecosystem & Partners

Connecting digital youth support with universities, public health authorities, and clinical hospitals in the Aix-Marseille region.

Aix-Marseille Université

Academic & Campus Wellness Nooks

Integrating QR-code “Wellness Nooks”, student orientation touchpoints, and peer ambassador networks across campus.

Agence Régionale de Santé (ARS)

Public Health & Therapy Subsidies

Co-funding no-cost therapy access for low-income youth and aligning preventive care with regional public health priorities.

Centre Hospitalier Montperrin

Clinical Safety Net

Providing immediate clinical escalation and specialized psychiatric support whenever acute crisis signals are detected.

Espoir Provence

Youth Community Outreach

Extending digital mental wellness tools beyond university campuses to reach underserved youth through local street teams.

French Federation of Psychiatry / Santé Mentale France

Clinical Guidance, Research & Anti-Stigma Advocacy

Supporting evidence-based self-help content design, ethical standards for youth AI tools, and regional mental health awareness initiatives.
